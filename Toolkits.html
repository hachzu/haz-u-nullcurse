CURSE TRACKER GUIDE

How To Use The Curse Tracker

The curse tracker reads your current level, player count, game mode, difficulty, and whichever enemies you have marked active, then shows you exactly which curses are available to pick at that combination of settings. Everything you click gets added to the Active Curses list in the sidebar, and the sidebar keeps a running Medal Payout total based on medal curses you pick from the dedicated Medal Curses pool.

Quick Steps

  1. CURRENT LEVEL
    15
    1Set your level
    Type your current level into the CURRENT LEVEL box at the top of the sidebar. Nearly everything else checks against that number, so it is worth setting first. Curses and enemies each have their own unlock level, and the pools will only show what you actually qualify for unless you turn on Show Upcoming or Show All Curses (Greater Curses are the one exception, covered further down).
  2. Solo
    Duo
    2Set your player count and game mode
    PLAYER COUNT and the GAME MODE buttons affect which curses are allowed. A couple of curses, like Mart Infection and Deadly Melody, are turned off entirely in Solo. Your player count also feeds into the Medal Payout math and the Shop Upgrade Calculator's prices, so it is worth keeping accurate even if you never touch a curse.
  3. Casual
    Extreme
    3Pick a difficulty
    Standard and Extreme show you the exact same curse pool. Extreme only changes prices over in the Shop Upgrade Calculator, it does not touch curses at all. Casual is the one that matters here: a handful of curses (things like Bigger Tripmines, More Tripmines, Minefield, Barotrauma, and the greater curse Run) are disabled outright on Casual. Switching difficulty never removes a curse you already picked under a different one, it only changes what you can add going forward.
  4. Bell
    4Mark your active enemies
    Down in ACTIVE ENEMIES, click whichever enemies are currently active. This unlocks every curse tied to that enemy. A few enemies show a small badge in their own corner because they need another enemy active first, Voidbound Baby needs two Babys, Voidbound Guardian needs a Guardian, and Baby and Husk specifically can be clicked again after that to stack up to two active at once. Some curses, like Husk Express, actually require that second stack.
  5. Fake Count
    Fake Count
    5Browse the pools and click to add a curse
    Scroll down to see four pools: Medal Curses at the very top for anything that pays out a bonus, then Global Curses, Enemy Curses, and Greater Curses. Under the default settings, only curses you actually qualify for right now appear at all, anything you are missing a level, an enemy, or a curse for simply is not in the list yet. Click a card once to add that curse; clicking it again does nothing unless the curse allows more than one stack, shown as a small "x / max" readout on the card.
  6. Random Spawn
    6Manage what you picked
    Everything you add lands in the ACTIVE CURSES list in the sidebar, with Greater Curses listed first, medal curses second, and everything else after that. Use the search box there to filter that list down by name (it only filters what you have already added, it does not touch the pools below), click the small × on an entry to remove just that one, use RESET RUN to wipe your level, difficulty, enemies, and curses back to a fresh start, or use REMOVE ALL CURSES to clear only the curses while leaving your level, mode, and enemies as they are.

Good To Know

◆
Greater Curses ignore level

The other three pools hide a curse completely the moment you fall short of a requirement. Greater Curses do not follow that rule for level: as long as an enemy-type Greater Curse's required enemy is active, it always shows up in the pool, even far below the level it unlocks at. It will just sit there dimmed with a level badge until you reach it, instead of disappearing and reappearing later. This lets you see and plan around Greater Curses well ahead of time without needing Show All Curses on for that one pool.

✕
Mutually exclusive curses

A handful of pairs can never both be active at once: High Roller and Tweaked Odds, Lap 2 and Fragile Gifts, Mart Infection and Mart Slide, Closer Husk and Further Husk, Ballet of Blades and Blade Bombardment. Picking one side normally makes the other disappear from its pool entirely. With Show All Curses on, the blocked side still renders dimmed but carries no badge explaining why, since the reason is not a level, an enemy, or another curse. With Unlock All Curses on, this exclusivity check is skipped entirely, the one dependency Unlock All does not enforce, so you can actually select both sides of a pair at once for planning purposes.

Picking Curses Efficiently

⚡
Plan a level or two ahead

Turn on SHOW UPCOMING before you start picking. It lists everything you would qualify for the moment your level ticks up, sorted soonest-first, so you can see what is about to open up instead of only reacting to what is available this second. This is especially useful for enemy curses, since those only preview once their enemy is already marked active.

⭐
Prioritize medal curses first

Medal curses picked from the dedicated MEDAL CURSES pool add straight to your MEDAL PAYOUT. When two curses are otherwise a toss-up, the one with the reward badge is usually the better pick. That pool pulls the highest-value options from Global and Enemy Curses together in one place instead of making you hunt through both.

🔄
Purify instead of removing

If a medal curse's payout is worth keeping but its actual effect is making the run harder than you'd like, do not remove it outright - open it in ACTIVE CURSES and hit Purify. You keep the Medal Payout slice it was already contributing, while its gameplay effect switches off. It costs Golden Gifts, so it is worth checking the price shown before committing.

📋
Sanity-check with Show All

Before locking in a run's curse pool, flip on SHOW ALL CURSES once to see the full picture, including anything still locked behind a level, enemy, or exclusive-group conflict. It's an easy way to catch a stronger pick you would have otherwise missed simply because it wasn't available yet.

Show Upcoming, Show All, Unlock All, and Hide Medal Curses

Four switches sit above the pools and control how much you see. Show Upcoming, Show All Curses, and Unlock All Curses are grouped under Reveal, going from a small peek ahead to everything at once. Hide Medal Curses sits on its own under Display.

SHOW UPCOMING

Adds the curses that only your level is holding back, listed after the ones you can pick right now, soonest first. An enemy curse only previews once its enemy is active (with the right count, so Husk Express needs two Husks), and a curse that needs another curse only previews once that curse is active. They show dimmed with a level badge and cannot be picked yet. Curses that will never appear on your difficulty or game mode stay hidden.

SHOW ALL CURSES

Reveals every curse in every pool, even ones you do not currently qualify for, so you can plan several levels ahead instead of only seeing what is available right now. This already includes everything Show Upcoming would add.

UNLOCK ALL CURSES

Only does something once Show All Curses is on, and stays disabled until then. Lets you select those locked curses with no level, enemy, curse, or exclusive-group requirement enforced. It never overrides a curse's own hard cap though, a curse already at its maximum stack still cannot be added again, and a curse disabled for your current game mode still cannot be selected either.

HIDE MEDAL CURSES

Hides the dedicated Medal Curses section at the top of the page. Curses that pay out a medal do not disappear, they just show up in their normal Global or Enemy Curses section instead, with that section’s colors and without a reward badge.

Medal Curses And Medal Payout

Curses marked with the cyan and pink highlight pay out a medal bonus while they are active. The MEDAL PAYOUT number in the sidebar, just under the difficulty buttons, adds together a base amount plus the value of every medal curse you select from the dedicated Medal Curses pool, scaled up by your player count. Picking that same curse from a Global or Enemy Curses pool applies its gameplay effect and can still be purified, but does not add a medal payout. Each medal curse's own reward badge shows the specific slice of that total it is responsible for, and that same number is also how many Golden Gifts you are rewarded for picking it from a medal curse pool. If you would rather keep a medal curse's payout without living with its downside, open it in the Active Curses list and press Purify.

Purifying a curse marks its entry gray and drops its actual effect out of your run, while still counting toward your Medal Payout. The tracker also shows the Golden Gift price you would pay to purify it. Press Purify again on a purified entry to undo it and go back to the curse being fully active, just note that if you had stacked the curse more than once before purifying it, un-purifying brings it back at a single stack, not the higher count it had before.
